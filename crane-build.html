Building a Motorised Crane

7 October 2026, academic day at WMG Academy

On 7 October we had an academic day at school, and we built a model crane from a laser-cut wooden kit. The crane has a small motor that winds a string to lift and lower a hook.

The Build

All the parts came as flat wooden panels. They slot together and are held with small nuts and bolts. The long arm of the crane (the jib) is made from a few panels joined into a box shape, and the tower and base are built the same way.

After the frame was done, we added the motor and the spool. The string goes from the spool along the jib to the hook, and a control box with two buttons moves the hook up and down.

Link to Physics

The triangle shapes cut into the jib make it stronger while keeping it light, because triangles don't change shape easily under a load. The shorter arm on the other side of the tower helps balance the crane, which links to moments in A-level Physics.
