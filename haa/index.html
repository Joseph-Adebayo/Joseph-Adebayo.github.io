01 / Web app · EdTechLive · 5 users

Revision Workspace

A revision web app for A-level students: annotate past papers with a stylus, mark your answers and track the mistakes you keep making. I built it because the tools I was using for my own revision didn't do this. Deployed on Vercel with a Supabase backend.

I don't know React or TypeScript yet, so I directed an AI coding agent to write the code. That includes the past-paper parser, which finds each question in an exam board's PDF and links it to the mark scheme. I designed the product, decided what to build next and tested it on my own revision.

Horowitz Andreessen Academy

Why I'm applying, and what I'll do with the year.

01 / The problem

What I want to build

I've experienced how AI can turn an idea into a working product without years of technical experience. But building something is only half the challenge; my failed e-commerce business taught me that finding real demand is just as important. I want to build AI tools that help first-time founders go from identifying a real problem to testing an idea, finding customers and launching a business, even without technical skills or connections.

02 / Why me

I already work this way

Nobody assigned me any of the work on this page. I spotted a gap in my own revision and shipped an app for it, using AI to write code in languages I haven't learned yet. I've run a business that failed and one that makes money, and I can explain why each went the way it did.

03 / Why HAA

The right room, early

I've learned to build by experimenting, failing and figuring things out on my own. At HAA, I want to work alongside ambitious founders, learn from people who've built successful companies and challenge myself to think bigger. I want to spend the year testing ideas with real customers, shipping products and finding a problem worth dedicating years to solving.
