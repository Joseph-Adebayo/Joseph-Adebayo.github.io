Experience

National Grid August 2026

I recently completed my Cyber Security Work Experience with National Grid, delivered through Generating Genius, and it completely changed how I think about protecting critical infrastructure.

Day 1 — Consultancy Challenge
We became "National Grid Cyber Security Consultants" for the day. Our challenge was to build and present a case explaining what National Grid does, why it matters, what the Great Grid Upgrade is, and why cyber security is essential to protecting it. It was a great introduction to working under pressure, meeting tight deadlines, collaborating as a team, and communicating technical ideas to a non-technical audience.

Day 2 — Cyber Awareness and the Escape Room
We heard directly from National Grid's Cyber Security Awareness and Resilience team before taking on a cyber security escape room. We had to crack ciphers, decode an IP address, identify a phishing email, and piece together CCTV and staff rota evidence to uncover an insider threat. What stood out to me was how the experience turned concepts like social engineering, phishing and incident investigation into something practical and genuinely engaging.

Day 3 — Eakring Site Visit
We travelled to the Eakring site to see the work in action. We met members of the team, toured the site, watched live demonstrations, and explored the differences between IT and OT security. Understanding that protecting a laptop or email account is very different from protecting the industrial control systems that help operate the electricity network gave me a completely different perspective on cyber security. We also explored some of the tools cyber security teams use, from endpoint protection to network monitoring, and discussed why security is so important when protecting the UK's critical national infrastructure.

This experience left me more informed, more curious, and even more certain that I want to build a future in technology.
A huge thank you to Generating Genius and everyone at National Grid for such a well-organised and genuinely eye-opening experience.

Rolls-Royce May 2026

I had an amazing opportunity to attend a 4-day behind-the-scenes work experience programme with Rolls-Royce in Solihull. It was an incredibly insightful experience that gave me a deeper understanding of the many departments that contribute to making the company what it is today.

Throughout the week, I had the chance to learn directly from employees and apprentices about their roles within the company, while also developing my technical understanding and professional skills.

One of the highlights was touring the Hydromech, Electronics and Test Engineering departments, which gave me first-hand insight into the day-to-day responsibilities of engineers. Alongside the tours, my team and I worked on a project focused on increasing STEM awareness, particularly in areas where exposure to STEM opportunities is limited.

This experience was truly inspiring and encouraged me to step out of my comfort zone, build confidence, and network with like-minded people. I'd like to thank everyone who organised and supported the programme, and to my group — it was a pleasure working with you all.
